Appendix 1: board mass and moment of inertia

The model is nondimensional: board mass mb = 1, g = 1, length unit L = 0.90 m (fully extended leg). The current OllieParams in ollie_hybrid.jl use deck_length 0.80 (0.72 m), wheelbase 0.58 (0.52 m), deck_height 0.08 (0.072 m), tail_rise 0.06 (0.054 m) and pitch inertia I = 0.085, i.e. ISI = 0.085 · mb · L2. This appendix checks those numbers against a real pro setup: Andy Anderson's Powell-Peralta Heron FLIGHT deck (shape 289) on Independent Stage 11 149 trucks (the truck spec Powell-Peralta ships on the Andy Anderson 8.45" complete; Anderson himself rides the equivalent Mini Logo 8.38" truck, which is ~347 g, so the conclusion is insensitive to the choice).

Kick-tail correction. The nose and tail (6.68" = 0.17 m each) are bent up by ~18°. Replacing the rod ends with inclined segments shortens their horizontal lever by (1 − cos 18°) ≈ 5 % and adds a vertical lever of ≤ 5 cm; the net change to Id is under 3 % and it lowers the total I by roughly 1 %, well inside the uncertainty of md. The deck contributes ~63 % of I and the trucks ~37 %, so the result is most sensitive to deck mass and length.

Comparison with the model

The model's I = 0.085 corresponds to 0.085·2.26·0.81 = 0.156 kg·m2, about 55 % higher than the 0.101 kg·m2 derived here. Part of the gap is the model geometry: its wheelbase (0.52 m) and deck (0.72 m) differ from the real 0.36 m / 0.81 m. Repeating the derivation with the model's geometry (rod 0.72 m, trucks at ±0.26 m) gives 0.050 + 0.075 + 0.002 = 0.127 kg·m2, i.e. I ≈ 0.069 nondimensional. So 0.085 is the right order of magnitude but on the high side either way.

Angular momentum used in the model. About the board COM, H = I ω with I = 0.055 (real geometry) in units of mbL2√(g/L). During the tail-on-ground phase the board pivots about the rear wheel: Ipivot = I + mbr2, where r is the COM-to-rear-axle distance, r2 = 0.1812 + 0.0312 = 0.0338 m2 (0.0417 nondim). Hence Ipivot = 0.101 + 2.262·0.0338 = 0.178 kg·m2, or 0.055 + 0.042 = 0.097 nondimensional (with the model's wheelbase, r2 = 0.262/0.81 = 0.083, Ipivot ≈ 0.15).

Recommendation

  • Board mass: 2.26 kg (deck 1.15 kg est. + 2 × 0.556 kg truck assemblies), matching the assumed 5 lb.
  • Mass ratio rider/board: 77.1/2.26 = 34.1; use rider_mass = 34.
  • Pitch inertia about COM: I = 0.055 nondimensional with the real deck/wheelbase; keep 0.07 if the current model geometry (wheelbase 0.58, deck 0.80) is retained. The existing 0.085 is ~25–55 % too high but not unreasonable given that grip tape, hardware and a heavier maple deck all push it upward.
